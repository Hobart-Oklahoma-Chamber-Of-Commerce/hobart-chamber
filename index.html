This Week's Feature

TH Rogers Lumber Co.

For generations, TH Rogers Lumber Co. has been more than a lumber yard — they've been a dependable partner and trusted resource for Hobart and Kiowa County. Their community footprint runs deep: supporting local schools, Relay for Life, the Kiowa County Junior Livestock Show, and this year providing all of the sand for the children's sandbox at Hobart's Celebration of Freedom. When severe weather hits, TH Rogers is stocked and ready — from salt in ice storms to roofing materials and special-order products for recovery. Leading the Hobart location is Brad Winkler, who has served TH Rogers for 35 years, alongside team members Brandon Tointigh, Michael Montgomery, Johnny Bullock, and Bernadette Bowen. Beyond lumber, they offer roofing, windows, doors, paint, tools, insulation, plumbing and electrical supplies, plus key cutting, glass cutting, and a maintained list of trusted local contractors. Congratulations to Brad and the entire TH Rogers team!

About the Chamber

Building a Vibrant Future for Hobart

The Hobart Chamber of Commerce is committed to supporting and promoting the businesses that make our community strong. Our mission is to encourage economic growth by connecting local businesses, attracting new opportunities, and creating a thriving environment for entrepreneurs and citizens alike.

We believe a strong business community leads to a strong Hobart. Through year-round events for adults, children, and families, we bring people together, drive traffic to local businesses, and showcase everything our town has to offer.
