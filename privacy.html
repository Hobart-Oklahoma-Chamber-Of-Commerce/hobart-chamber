Hobart Chamber of Commerce

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

This policy covers hobartchamber.org and the automated posting tool the Hobart Chamber of Commerce ("the Chamber," Hobart, Oklahoma) uses to publish its own events and member news to the Chamber's Facebook Page.

What the posting tool does

The tool reads public information already on hobartchamber.org, such as events, member listings and restaurant menus. It then publishes posts to the Hobart Chamber of Commerce Facebook Page. It does not read, collect or store information about people who visit, follow or comment on the Page.

What we collect

  • The posting tool: no personal data about visitors, followers or commenters. It stores only a log of which Chamber posts have already been published, so the same post is not sent twice.
  • The website: contact details you choose to submit through a form, such as a registration or membership inquiry. We use them only to respond to you and run the Chamber's programs.

Facebook

The Chamber's Page is run on Facebook. Your use of Facebook is governed by Meta's own privacy policy and terms. The Chamber does not receive your Facebook profile information through this tool.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help run the website, and only as needed to operate it, or when the law requires.

Data deletion

To ask us to delete information you submitted to the Chamber, email the Chamber and include your name and what you want removed. We will respond and delete it unless we are legally required to keep it.

Contact

Hobart Chamber of Commerce, Hobart, Oklahoma 73651. Reach us through the contact details on hobartchamber.org.
