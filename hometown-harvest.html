Whether you're showing a tractor, marching in the parade, setting up a booth, or bringing the kids — here's everything you need to get signed up.

Tractor Show
$20 Entry Fee

Antique and classic tractors on display at the Courthouse Square. Show off your piece of history and compete for recognition.

  • Antique & classic tractors welcome
  • $20 registration fee
  • Display on the Courthouse Square
Register Your Tractor →

Community Parade
Free to Enter

March, ride, or roll in the Hometown Harvest Community Parade through downtown Hobart. Everyone is welcome.

  • Open to all — march, ride, or roll
  • Floats, vehicles, groups, bands
  • No entry fee
Join the Parade →

Vendor / Booth
Limited Spots

Set up your booth on the Courthouse Square alongside food trucks and other vendors for one of Hobart's biggest fall events.

  • Craft vendors, food, retail
  • Prime downtown foot traffic
  • Limited spaces — register early
Vend Here →
