Hobart's Hometown Harvest · September 19, 2026

Vendor Registration

Set up on the Kiowa County Courthouse Square alongside food trucks, artisans, and local businesses. Vendors are limited — register early to secure your spot.

📍 316 S Main · Courthouse Square 📅 September 19, 2026 · 10 AM 🚛 Setup Begins 8 AM
Before You Register — Please Read This year's Hometown Harvest features a tractor show, silent auction, music, parade, and much more. The event runs from 10:00 AM — setup begins at 8:00 AM. Vendors will unload on the east side of the square. We are limiting vendors and food trucks to avoid duplicate product categories so that every vendor has a profitable day. Space fees are due prior to the event — we accept cash, check, or card. Detailed instructions will be sent by email closer to the event. Questions? Call (580) 682-0887 or email director@hobartokchamber.org.
